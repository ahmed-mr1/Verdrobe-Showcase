## 3. LinkedIn Post (Standard Version · ~180 words)

> Buying pre-loved clothing online is great for sustainability, but sizing anxiety is real: vintage tags are inconsistent, different brands fit differently, and peer-to-peer sellers can't accept returns.
> 
> To solve this, I built **Verdrobe**—a second-hand fashion marketplace that projects flat-lay garment measurements directly onto your personal 3D body avatar as an intuitive fit map.
> 
> Rather than relying on slow, buggy 3D cloth physics, Verdrobe takes a pragmatic engineering approach:
> - **Parametric 3D Body**: An open-source Anny/FastAPI microservice fits a rigged human mesh to user measurements with **0.357 cm median accuracy** (< 4 mm error).
> - **Real-Time Fit Heatmap**: The fit engine calculates ease (garment circumference minus body circumference) and paints contiguous color bands in Three.js at 60 FPS (Red = tight, Green = balanced, Blue = loose).
> - **E-Commerce Architecture**: Built with Next.js 14, Tailwind, and Prisma with atomic transactions preventing double-buys, backed by 36 automated unit and contrast tests.
